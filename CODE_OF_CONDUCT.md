# Code of Conduct

## My Commitment

I am committed to building a welcoming and inclusive environment for everyone who participates in Honeypot Kit, regardless of background, experience level, identity, or perspective.

I believe learning happens best in safe spaces where people can ask questions, experiment, fail, and try again without fear of judgment.

## Values I'm Building

These are the values that guide Honeypot Kit's community:

**Respect:** Treat everyone with dignity. Disagree thoughtfully. Learn from different viewpoints.

**Curiosity:** Questions are welcome. No such thing as a dumb question in a learning space.

**Collaboration:** We succeed together. Help others when you can. Ask for help when you need it.

**Inclusion:** I welcome people from all backgrounds. I actively work to include underrepresented voices. I create space for different ways of learning and contributing.

**Honesty:** Be truthful about what you know and don't know. Acknowledge mistakes and learn from them. Share your actual experience.

**Professionalism:** Keep interactions constructive. Focus on ideas, not people. Disagree respectfully.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional in all interactions
- Welcome new contributors and help them get oriented
- Ask questions if something is unclear (clarifying is helpful for everyone)
- Share knowledge generously
- Give credit where due
- Provide constructive feedback focused on ideas, not people
- Listen actively to others' perspectives
- Be patient with different skill levels and backgrounds
- Focus module contributions on actual functionality and learning value
- Avoid excessive marketing language or vendor promotion in modules
- Be honest about what a module does and doesn't do
- Discuss tradeoffs openly rather than overselling features
- Keep technical discussions grounded in reality, not hype

## Unacceptable Behavior

We do not tolerate:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination based on any protected characteristic
- Insulting or demeaning language, especially related to identity
- Sexual or violent content or threats
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Doxxing or sharing private information without consent
- Deliberate disruption or trolling
- Continuing behavior after someone asks you to stop

Unacceptable behavior applies across all project spaces: GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, comments, and any related communication channels.

## Reporting and Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

1. **Document it**: Save relevant comments, messages, or details
2. **Report it**: Email burns@interhouse.com with details of what happened
3. **What happens next**: Reports will be reviewed and responded to within 7 days

I take all reports seriously and will:
- Investigate fairly and confidentially
- Work to resolve the issue
- Take appropriate action (which may include warnings, suspension, or removal from the project)

Reports are handled by me and will not be publicly disclosed without consent of the reporter.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All project repositories
- Issue trackers and pull requests
- GitHub discussions and comments
- Commit messages
- Any space representing the Honeypot Kit project

## Inspiration

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant v2.1 and tailored for the Honeypot Kit community.

I recognize that different communities have different norms. This code reflects what I value: learning, respect, and inclusion.

## Questions?

If you have questions about this Code of Conduct or how it applies in a specific situation, reach out to burns@interhouse.com.
